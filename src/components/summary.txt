Operational Framework & Project FoundationsGoogle Business Profile (GBP) functions as a core utility enabling businesses and organizations to systematically oversee their digital footprints across Google Search and Google Maps. The primary operational objective of this moderation workflow is to evaluate edits and creations performed on claimed listings by listing owners—internally referred to as GAIAs (Google Account & ID Administration). Each registered email address is bound to a unique numerical GAIA ID, which constitutes Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and must strictly never be shared, copied, or exposed across unencrypted or public channels.The underlying pipeline relies on a definitive progression to maintain geographic data purity: a listing is claimed or created by a GAIA, edits are pushed to the queue, operators conduct multi-layered fraud/policy reviews to identify systemic spam, and a final binary or conditional action is applied to generate a clean ecosystem. Legitimate listings represent a distinct Point of Interest (POI) that facilitates user navigation by providing structural attributes such as categories, coordinates, physical addresses, and active communication channels. Examples include public utilities, landmarks, and commercial entities like schools, libraries, hospitals, parks, or restaurants.Internal Moderation Tools & Technical ProceduresOperators utilize an integrated suite of internal tools and dashboards to cross-examine account validity and listing integrity:Firebolt: The primary interface utilized to pull active tasks from assigned queues, evaluate listing metadata, and log final modification verdicts.SuperMario: A geographic data debugging tool designed to trace backend protocol buffers. Operators cross-verify historical changes by locating a task in Firebolt and clicking the primary + Full Proto link at the base of the interface to extract the unique underlying Listing_id.BB Admin (Biz Builder): A profile investigation dashboard structurally divided into a Left Hand Side Panel, a Main Section, and a Right Hand Side Panel. This tool aggregates verification events, history logs, and account relationship maps to expose merchant intent.Pushpin: A dedicated map editing interface accessed through internal links to review physical marker placement, layout adjustments, and precise spatial coordinate mapping.Ustad (Spam Trends Dashboard): A real-time data visualization engine that aggregates active regional fraud spikes, watchlists, and coordinated keyword trends.To begin an active audit, operators must execute a precise five-step click-path within Firebolt:Click on the Review Type field.Select the targeted, appropriate workflow review parameters.Click Fetch to ping the server.Select the designated operational Queue.Click Start Review to initialize the ticket.Module 1: Permitted Places Check (Policy Eligibility)The initial gate of the workflow dictates that any map feature must reflect three core pillars: it must be Relevant, Legal, and Public. Non-permitted features that face immediate deletion include natural anomalies or non-commercial private items (e.g., individual trees or generic private residences).

Legal Compliance & Structural RestrictionsListings must fully comply with localized regional laws. Operators check constraints via go/geo-illegal-gcids or secondary references like Wikipedia; for example, adult brothels in Canada or erotic massage categories in France are illegal and must be rejected.Security protocols strictly forbid overly detailed sub-features of military installations, such as individual barracks or mess halls. Data centers are prohibited unless their location is explicitly validated via an official corporate website Authority Page (AP) or distinct building signage on Street View. Physical properties listed purely for sale or rent, alongside vacation sublets or seasonal homes operating without a permanent on-site management office, are barred.Local Service Business (LSB) & E-Commerce HandlingFor Local Service Businesses (LSBs) operating without a physical storefront, the listing must feature either a valid phone number or an active, relevant website that contains clear contact options like forms, direct phone lines, or automated chatbots. If an LSB contains neither a phone nor a website, it is non-permitted. Automated platforms powered by Google (business.site or negocio.site), unapproved third-party aggregators, or non-functional broken domains cannot be counted as valid contact resources.Pure online businesses lacking physical localized interaction points (e.g., virtual tutoring platforms like Udemy or Unacademy, remote website hosting companies, or video-only medical consultations) are completely non-permitted. For product-based E-commerce or LSB listings, operators must evaluate the defined service boundaries on BB Admin or the merchant's site. If delivery zones or service footprints expand beyond a single state's boundaries, the listing is rejected. However, small geographic territories (e.g., Singapore, Maldives) serve as explicit exceptions where country-wide service configurations are permitted due to restricted physical terrain size.Naming Conventions & Prank GuardrailsBusiness names consisting of unverified profanity ("Royal cafe - Bastard"), completely random gibberish strings ("hcqbgwiyexw"), or vague numerical codes ("12345") are restricted. If a business is physically real but the owner has violated formatting standards—such as stuffing titles with marketing slogans, emojis, special characters, URLs, email addresses, phone numbers, or more than three descriptors—the listing must not be rejected. Instead, operators must leave an internal comment stating "Escalate to BRF" to route the item to the Business Redressal Form (BRF) team, who will systematically standardize the name using Pushpin.Prank listings are identified when prominent, high-profile infrastructure categories are placed at obviously fraudulent locations (e.g., marking an international airport over a suburban house, dropping a local cafe pin directly over the Eiffel Tower or Taj Mahal, or setting an embassy marker in an unpopulated desert). However, context-appropriate placements—such as positioning a fish farm, water park, or water treatment plant directly over a water body coordinate—must never be flagged as spam. Call centers or hotlines that do not facilitate face-to-face assistance are banned unless they operate as physical, signed customer support hubs. Temporary listings representing transient events (e.g., commercial festivals, temporary pop-up clearance sales, movie releases, or weddings) are non-permitted. Internal store segments, individual aisles, loading docks, or specific facility access gates must be restricted as independent listings and integrated as attributes under the primary parent building.High-Risk Content & Safety EscalationsStrict enforcement mandates zero tolerance for Child Freedom/Safety violations and Violent Extremism:CSAI: Child Sexual Abuse Imagery or explicit visual exploitation of minors dictates an immediate rejection, combined with a mandatory escalation via go/contact-child-safety. Exceptions apply strictly to valid medical, scientific, artistic, or educational contexts (such as breastfeeding).Violent Extremism: Content displaying ultra-graphic violence (such as active beheadings), material soliciting terror funds, or listings using symbolic graphics representing restricted groups must be shut down. Operators cross-examine suspicious emblems using the internal database at go/terroristlogos.Module 2: Existence & Resources Check (Data Validation)The verification of a listing's real-world physical operation relies on a rigid structural hierarchy of resource classification:Approved/Permitted ResourcesAuthority Page (AP): The official standalone corporate domain owned directly by the business. The "Contact Us" sub-page must explicitly display a matching physical localized address and phone number.Street View (SV): The primary visual verification baseline. Operators use Pushpin or the BB Admin map preview to review current published and unpublished imagery (enabled via Show -> Unpublished Imagery), checking cross-street views, seasonal captures, and building unit numbers to confirm permanent, non-morphed structural signage.Custom Search Snippets (go/cuse): An internal query engine that filters out blacklisted directories or paid ads. A business name and complete address string must be copied exactly via the BB Admin ID card copy icon without any manually entered alterations. If matching details appear within 3 or more distinct snippets from 3 or more separate domains, it serves as an approved existence signal.Third-Party Platforms: Includes verified Wikipedia pages, official State Government corporate registration repositories, and accredited Better Business Bureau profiles (bbb.org) strictly for United States and Canadian markets.Social Media Assets: Facebook and Instagram business pages are accepted only if the direct hyperlinks are pre-populated within the BB Admin tool interface; operators are strictly banned from manual web searches for social accounts. The destination profile must match the target name and address, and pass an active user engagement threshold requiring at least 10 independent posts AND at least 30 active followers. If the profile requests login credentials to view standard business info, the resource must be skipped.Unapproved ResourcesUnapproved resources can only be used to collect structural context or cross-check abuse indicators within Onboarding 2A and 3A queues. They can never be used as sole proof to issue an "Accept" or "Leaning Accept" verdict. These consist of generic Google Search landing engine result strings or go/cuse search returns that yield fewer than three independent domain snippets.Prohibited ResourcesProhibited assets are entirely banned due to data rights restrictions or systemic unreliability. These include competitor mapping platforms, user-generated review aggregators, legacy Google+ pages, front-end public maps.google.com user views, sites featuring "Powered by Google" or "Google Technology" footprints in their footers, automatically generated Google Business Sites (domains ending in .business.site or .negocio.site), and generic templated websites that contain no unique contextual data beyond an isolated address and business name placeholder string.Technical Note on Web Errors: If an approved merchant link throws a "Connection is not private" SSL security block during an audit, operators bypass the warning panel by selecting Advanced -> Proceed to the website, or utilize the Polyjuice Proxy browser extension to tunnel into region-locked international sites.Module 3: Positive Checks & Abuse PatternsPositive checks evaluate historical data on BB Admin to rule out fraudulent intent. If a listing shows a historic log marker indicating it was successfully Verified by Ops (applicable strictly to non-onboarding workflows) or if the specific GAIA account string is listed on an internal Allowlist/Whitelist, the system assumes a low probability of fraud, allowing the operator to proceed.Coordinated Spam & Trend IdentificationOperators monitor active fraud campaigns by applying explicit filters within the Ustad dashboard, isolating the target country code alongside "GLOBAL" parameters, and sorting states by "Active", "On Going", or "Null". If a listing under review exhibits a structural match of greater than 50% against an active trend's primary parameters (e.g., generating gibberish text fields, using automated categories like "yeshiva", or omitting standard phone numbers) while completely lacking valid verifications, it is flagged as Abuse. Any newly identified, repeating patterns that are missing from the dashboard must be documented and escalated to team Leads immediately.Suspicious Identity Changes CheckOperators execute deep audits by selecting the T4B TAB - ID CHANGE module on the right-hand menu of BB Admin, applying the "Edits by Owner or Manager" script filter, and selecting "Expand All Edits". This ledger exposes post-creation modifications applied to three critical targets: Address, SAB Type, and Primary Phone fields.

This offline verification method allows merchants to record unedited video evidence to secure account activation. Operators locate these files by navigating to Debug View -> Verification Events -> Click Video.


BVV Technical Criteria & Core Pillars

To serve as an approved verification resource, a BVV must be a single, continuous, completely unedited recording. It must fulfill four operational criteria:BVV PillarRequirements & Evidence


BVV Pillar,Requirements & Evidence
1. Spatial Location,"Must display clear real-world landmarks, abundant street signs, or building numbers that precisely match Street View or aerial satellite layout grids."
2. Physical Existence,"Must visually document a permanent storefront, external structural signage, or internal branding elements matching the target entity."
3. Owner Affiliation,"The claimant must visually prove operational control by unlocking physical entry points, accessing restricted rear employee offices, or opening the localized Point of Sale (POS) machine."
4. Tools of Trade,"Mandatory for Locksmith, Plumber, and Roofing Contractor categories. The video must clearly show professional equipment, branded employee uniforms, or commercial service vehicles."

Videos showing properties under active structural construction, open barren fields, or simulated 3D digital renderings are prohibited. Operators evaluate physical proximity via the straight-line Crow Fly distance method by right-clicking the core location anchor dot on the tracking map and measuring directly to the target destination coordinate.If a video confirms the recording was shot in a completely separate city or area outside a standard baseline, it fails anchoring and must be rejected. Discrepancies falling within a tight 1 km radius are passed for anchoring, but must still be rejected and escalated to the queue supervisor.Video Abuse & Escalation PathIf a BVV contains egregious violations—including child exploitation (CSAI), explicit adult media, extreme profanity, graphic gore, or active terrorist propaganda—operators must initiate a multi-threaded high-priority escalation path:Apply a definitive block to the listing layout and report the file details directly to the queue Lead.The Lead logs a high-priority P0 bug targeting the Abuse Ops team via the tracking component path: Geo > Data > MapSpam > Takedowns.The Lead simultaneously opens a cross-reference ticket targeting the Commerce Local Trust team via the component path: Geo > Marketplaces > Merchant Platform > Trust, explicitly CCing the primary monitoring aliases: geo-spam-ops@google.com and msa-global+bugs@google.com.Queue-Specific Evaluation MatricesOperational handling rules shift fundamentally based on the assigned risk profile of the work queue:Onboarding 2A & 3A (Low to Mid-Risk)Onboarding queues do not accept raw, new LSB profile creations; entries created as an LSB or changed to an HSB/SF post-verification must be marked Indecisive-Indecisive. Operators verify post-verification states by checking the "All Biz Builder Events" ledger under the profile History section; if the "Verification Type" variable explicitly states "PIN entered and accepted", the verification was completed.If a listing shifts from an LSB to an HSB before verification occurs, operators can proceed with standard modules. In low-to-mid-risk queues, a BVV can be used in three distinct paths: to accept a listing if it verifies standard contact details (phone/email matching the domain), to confirm physical owner affiliation, or to establish basic existence in the absence of conflicting fraud signals.Important Queue Exception (3A): In the Onboarding 3A queue, BVV files are completely unavailable. Operators rely entirely on standard web research modules and historical text logs, and skip LSB transition rules since the queue exclusively targets low-to-medium-risk service business profiles.Onboarding 2B, 3B, 4A, & 4B (Mid to High-Risk)In these specialized pilot queues, the data evaluation rules become more restrictive. A BVV can only be used in two specific ways: it must positively verify a trivial phone/email domain ID or it must prove direct physical owner affiliation. General proof of real-world physical existence alone is insufficient to grant an approval.Onboarding 2C, 3C, & 4C (High-Risk Hubs)These queues manage high-risk storefronts, service providers, and suspect reverification fallbacks. The operational rules mandate a strict, uncompromising protocol:Mandatory BVV Validation: Every single profile must possess an active verification video. If the video is missing, or if an internal server error prevents playback, the operator must immediately halt review and mark the task Indecisive-Indecisive without running any secondary modules.Resource Restrictions: The usage of unapproved secondary resources is prohibited. The transitional verdict Indecisive - Leaning Accept is entirely disabled.Strict Adjudication Path: A task can only return three final codes:Reject: If any single abuse check returns a positive indicator, if anchoring parameters fail, or if the BVV is flagged as junk or abusive.Indecisive-Indecisive: If physical existence and owner affiliation parameters remain unverified, or if the video is completely missing.Accept: Granted strictly when both physical real-world existence and direct merchant affiliation are completely verified via the BVV. For high-risk service profiles (LSBs), paper documentation or systemic registry listings are accepted only if their authenticity is verified by the video data.
